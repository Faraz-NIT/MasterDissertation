# Rewritten Chapters 1 and 2: literature review

Download the [Word document](Chapters_1_and_2_Literature_Review.docx) or the
[18-page reading PDF](Chapters_1_and_2_Literature_Review.pdf).
Both contain **only Chapters 1 and 2**, including the research questions and hypotheses
within Chapter 2. They are a standalone revision for insertion into the dissertation.

Chapter 1 reviews retail replenishment, information quality, forecasting, supply
uncertainty and perishability, including the complementary roles of M5 and
FreshRetailNet-50K. Chapter 2 reviews tool-using LLM agents, multiple-agent workflows,
automation and evaluation, then states the research gap, main question, four
supporting questions and hypotheses H1–H4. H1 and H2 each distinguish service and cost.
The hypotheses are an analytical framework; the text makes no claim of preregistration.

The two chapters contain 5,824 prose words excluding headings. Main-body formatting
uses Times New Roman, 12 points, 1.5 spacing, justified text and one-inch A4 margins.
The documents reproduce the delivered files byte for byte. This revision introduces
no new experiments or results.
